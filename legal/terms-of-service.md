## 1. About the App

Whisper to Invoice helps small businesses create invoices using voice input and on-device artificial intelligence. The App can:

- Record spoken sale descriptions via your device microphone
- Extract invoice fields using an on-device AI model (Gemma)
- Store business profile and invoice data locally on your device
- Generate PDF invoices that you may share through other apps

After an initial one-time download of the AI model, core invoice features are designed to work **offline on your device**.
