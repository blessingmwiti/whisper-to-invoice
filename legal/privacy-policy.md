## 3. Information the App processes

### 3.1 Microphone audio

When you create an invoice by voice, the App accesses your device **microphone** to record your spoken description of a sale.

- Audio is processed **on your device** using an on-device AI model (Gemma) to extract invoice fields.
- Audio is **not automatically uploaded** to Sawiti Tech servers.
- We do not retain voice recordings on our servers because we do not receive them.

### 3.2 Business profile

You may enter and store:

- Business name and tagline  
- Address  
- Phone number and email  
- Payment instructions (e.g. M-Pesa or bank details)  
- Optional business logo image  

This information appears on invoices you generate.

### 3.3 Invoice and client data

The App stores invoice content you create or edit, including:

- Client names  
- Line items (description, quantity, unit price, totals)  
- Dates, currency, tax, and notes  
- Generated PDF invoices  

### 3.4 App preferences

We store local preferences such as:

- Theme selection (light / dark / auto)  
- Whether you accepted the Terms of Service  

### 3.5 Information we do **not** collect

In the current version of Whisper to Invoice, we do **not**:

- Require you to create a Sawiti Tech account  
- Collect analytics or advertising identifiers  
- Track your location  
- Access your contacts, SMS, or call logs  
- Automatically upload invoices or voice recordings to our servers

## 5. Where data is stored

All business profile, invoice, and preference data is stored **locally on your device** in the App’s private storage.

We do not operate a backend database that stores your invoice content for this App.

**Device backup:** We have disabled Android cloud backup for this App to reduce the risk of invoice data being copied to cloud backup services. Data remains on your device unless you share it.

---

## 6. When information leaves your device

Information is transmitted off your device only in these situations:

### 6.1 When you share an invoice

If you share a PDF invoice via WhatsApp, email, or another app, that transfer is handled by the app you choose. We do not control how the recipient uses shared invoices. Review the third party’s privacy policy before sharing.

### 6.2 AI model download

A **one-time internet connection** is required to download the on-device AI model. This download is initiated by you and does not include your invoice content.

### 6.3 Support email

If you contact us at info@sawititech.co.ke, we receive whatever information you choose to include in your message.

---

## 7. On-device artificial intelligence

Invoice fields may be generated by an on-device **Gemma** model. Processing happens locally; your spoken input is not sent to Sawiti Tech for cloud inference.

AI output may be inaccurate. Always review invoices before sharing.

Use of Gemma is also subject to Google’s [Gemma Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms).

- **On our servers:** We do not store your invoice or voice data on our servers in the ordinary operation of this App.
